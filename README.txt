==================================================
PROJECT OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURE
==================================================
This website is a production-grade, mobile-first multi-page e-commerce website for Mithai Mixture India Private Limited.

Pages Included (19 Pages):
- index.html            Cinematic Home Page (Hero, categories, bestsellers, promises, story, CTA)
- categories.html       Category Hub (Overview of 8 distinct departments)
- shop.html             Shop All (Search, Sort, Category Filter chips, 66 products)
- sweets.html           Traditional Sweets (28 menu items + 5 packaged sweet assortments)
- biscuits.html         Premium Biscuits (Mithai, Osmania, Jeera, Marie)
- beverages.html        Mithai Beverages (Mithai Badam 350ml)
- mixtures.html         Signature Mixtures (Khatta Meetha, Moong Dal, Navratan, Punjabi Tadka, Bombay)
- pickles.html          Traditional Pickles (Mango, Lime, Chilli, Garlic, Chicken, Mutton, Prawn, Fish)
- chocolates.html       Premium Chocolates (Truffles, Pralines, Assortments, Nut Chocolates)
- frozen-foods.html     Frozen & Ready-to-Cook (Samosa, Momos, Kebabs, Tikka, Spring Rolls, etc.)
- rates.html            Complete 28-Sweet Rate List (Transparent ₹/kg table, quick +ADD buttons)
- about.html            Our Story & Heritage (Quality pillars, tradition)
- contact.html          Contact Us (WhatsApp link & interactive customer enquiry form)
- order.html            Order Checkout (Full review, kg/pack modifiers, customer details form)
- success.html          Order Confirmed Screen (Order ID, itemized receipt, WhatsApp re-connect)
- privacy.html          Privacy Policy
- terms.html            Terms & Conditions
- shipping.html         Shipping & Delivery Policy
- refunds.html          Refund & Cancellation Policy
- mixtures-pickles.html Combined savouries hub

Directory Structure:
/css/style.css          Master luxury stylesheet with deep maroon, antique gold and responsive mobile layouts
/js/config.js           Single-point central configuration (WhatsApp number, Google Sheets URL)
/js/products-data.js    Centralized 66-item product catalogue with exact prices and WebP mappings
/js/cart.js             Shopping cart engine with 0.25 kg math, item removal, drawer & localStorage
/js/api.js              Google Sheets backend integration with resilient 6s timeout & offline fallback
/js/app.js              Product quick-view modal, live search, sort, category filters & checkout
/assets/                73 high-resolution WebP product photographs, category banners and logo
Code.gs                 Google Apps Script backend web app (handles products, orders, enquiries)
robots.txt & sitemap.xml SEO metadata

==================================================
DEPLOYMENT
==================================================
The frontend is 100% static and requires no Node.js or local backend server.
Deploy directly to Vercel, Netlify, or GitHub Pages.
Backend functionality is powered seamlessly via Google Apps Script (Code.gs).
