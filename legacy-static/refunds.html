Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Order Cancellation

Because our traditional sweets, savouries and delicacies are freshly prepared and packaged, orders can only be cancelled or modified prior to preparation and dispatch. Please inform us immediately via WhatsApp (+91 81252 13332) if you need to adjust your order.

2. Perishable Food Items

In compliance with FSSAI regulations and food safety standards, edible goods and perishable sweets cannot be returned once delivered and unsealed, unless damaged or defective.

3. Damage or Quality Issues

Customer satisfaction is our utmost tradition. In the rare event that an item arrives damaged during transit or fails to meet our high quality standard, please notify us on WhatsApp within 6 hours of delivery along with a photograph. We will promptly arrange a replacement or refund as appropriate.

4. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed via original payment mode (UPI, bank transfer) within 2-4 business days.
