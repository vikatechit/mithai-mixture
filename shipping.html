Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Fresh Daily Preparation

To uphold our motto “Taste with Tradition”, our sweets and savouries are prepared fresh in hygienic kitchen facilities. Orders are packed in food-grade, tamper-evident containers to preserve their aroma, crispness and freshness.

2. Local & Regional Delivery

We offer prompt local delivery for fresh sweets, beverages and frozen foods, as well as courier dispatch for dry savouries, pickles and packaged biscuits across supported pin codes.

3. Delivery Charges

Delivery charges vary based on distance, order volume, and whether express or cold-chain delivery is required for perishable sweets. The exact delivery fee is communicated transparently to you via WhatsApp prior to order dispatch.

4. Order Tracking & WhatsApp Updates

Upon order submission, you will receive an Order ID (e.g. MM-YYYYMMDD-XXXX). Our fulfillment team will keep you updated via WhatsApp regarding dispatch, rider tracking, or tracking numbers for courier consignments.
